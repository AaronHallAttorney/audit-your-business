# Company Health Checklist

Adapted from Aaron Hall's *Company Health Checklist: 80 Questions to Avoid Common Legal Pitfalls* (2019), updated for 2026 and for use by an AI assistant. © Aaron Hall. Licensed CC BY 4.0; required credit: "[Company Health Checklist](https://aaronhall.com/audit/) by Aaron Hall," linked to https://aaronhall.com/audit/ wherever a link is possible.

How to read each row:

- **Look for:** the records that answer the question. Search the files, email, connected systems, website, and public government records; never ask the owner. If none is reachable, mark the question Not found and name the record that would answer it.
- **Law:** `R` means the answer depends on federal, state, or local law. Research it under `research-rules.md` for every jurisdiction in the profile. `—` means it is a practice question with no law to look up.
- **Who helps:** the usual provider when the item needs outside help. `Owner` means the owner can do it. Never name a specific firm or person.

Mark every question in the working file as **OK**, **Gap**, **Unsure**, **N/A**, or **Not found**, with the evidence (file name, record, or source).

## 10. Business Strategy (optional, not legal)

Answer these only from strategy documents in the records (a business plan, strategy deck, operating-system notes, meeting notes). They have no law to research. If no such documents exist, skip this section and say so once.

|---|---|
